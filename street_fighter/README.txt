STREET FIGHTER - CONTROLS
=========================

PLAYER 1:
  Move:         W (up), A (left), S (down), D (right)
  Attack:       1 / 2 / 3 (light / medium / heavy)
  Alt moves:    Q + 1/2/3
  Super:        E

PLAYER 2 (2-Player mode):
  Move:         Arrow keys
  Attack:       J / K / L (light / medium / heavy)
  Alt moves:    U + J/K/L
  Super:        I
