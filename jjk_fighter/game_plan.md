# JUJUTSU KAISEN: CURSE CLASH - ULTIMATE GAME PLAN

## VISION
A full-featured 2D fighting game with Story Mode, AI opponents, unlockable content, deep combat mechanics, and 10+ characters with unique curse technique movesets.

## 3. ENERGY & COMBAT SYSTEMS

### 3.1 Cursed Energy (CE)
- **Max**: 100 (base), upgradable via story
- **Regen**: 0.3/sec normally, 0.1/sec while blocking, 0.5/sec on hit
- **Costs**: Light 0, Heavy 0, Special 15-50, Ultimate 100
- **Overflow**: If CE hits 200% (via pickups/story buff), attacks do 1.3x damage

### 3.2 Black Flash Mechanic
- 5% chance on any attack to trigger Black Flash
- Deals 2.5x damage + screen flash + unique VFX
- Story progress increases chance to 12% after Ch5
- Certain moves guarantee Black Flash (Yuji's Binding Vow)

### 3.3 Combo System
- **Hitstun**: Light = 12f, Heavy = 18f, Special = 22f
- **Juggle**: Launch attacks send airborne, follow with aerial
- **Cancel**: Light → Heavy → Special (chain window 8f)
- **Burst**: Spend 50 CE to break out of enemy combo (once per round)

### 3.4 Defense
- **Block**: Hold S. Reduces damage 80%. Chip damage exists.
- **Parry**: Tap S within 4f of hit. Full negate + 15 CE gain.
- **Dodge**: Double-tap back. 10f invincibility. Costs 10 CE.
- **Burst**: Spend 50 CE during combo to escape.

### 3.5 Domain Expansion
- Requires 100 CE (full meter)
- 1.5s startup (can be interrupted)
- Unique effect per character (stun, damage, buff)
- Lasts 5-8 seconds
- Opponent can counter-domain if they also have 100 CE

## 4. AI SYSTEM

### 4.1 Difficulty Levels

| Level | Reaction Time | Combo Skill | Meter Use | Behavior |
|-------|--------------|-------------|-----------|----------|
| Easy | 20f delay | 2-hit combos | Rare | Random, blocks rarely |
| Normal | 12f delay | 3-hit combos | Sometimes | Blocks, punishes mistakes |
| Hard | 6f delay | 4-hit combos | Smart | Reads inputs, baits, combos |
| Cursed | 2f delay | Optimal combos | Perfect | Frame-perfect, adapts |

### 4.2 AI Behaviors
- **Neutral**: Dash in, poke, retreat based on character archetype
- **Offense**: Execute optimal BnB combos, use meter on confirms
- **Defense**: Block high/low mixups, parry on Hard+, use Burst
- **Adaptation**: After 3 same moves, AI starts countering
- **Personality**: Each character AI uses their moveset preferentially

### 4.3 Boss AI
- Story bosses have unique patterns:
  - Mahito: Transfigures after 50% HP (new moveset)
  - Sukuna: Taunts, uses Dismantle randomly, Malevolent Shrine at 25% HP
  - Jogo: Opens with Meteor, zones with insects

## 5. PROGRESSION SYSTEM

### 5.1 Currency: Cursed Points (CP)
- Earned from fights: Win = 50 CP, Loss = 15 CP, Perfect = +30 CP
- Story bonus: First clear = 100 CP

### 5.2 Unlockables

| Category | Items | Cost |
|----------|-------|------|
| Characters | 4 base (Yuji, Gojo, Sukuna, Megumi), 8 unlockable | 200-500 CP |
| Techniques | Alternate colors, enhanced versions | 100-300 CP |
| Stages | 6 backgrounds | 150 CP each |
| Music | JJK OST tracks (procedural placeholders) | 50 CP |
| Gallery | Character bios, technique lore | 25 CP |

### 5.3 Player Progression
- **Player Level**: XP from fights. Level up = 50 CP bonus.
- **Character Mastery**: Use a character 50 times = Mastery Skin
- **Achievements**: First Domain, 10-win streak, Perfect round, etc.

## 7. VISUAL EFFECTS SPEC

### 7.1 Particle Categories
- **Energy Trails**: Colored glow particles following attacks
- **Impact Burst**: Radial burst on hit (white flash + colored sparks)
- **Special Effects**: Character-specific (fire, blood, shadows, lightning)
- **Domain Overlay**: Full-screen tint + floating elements
- **UI Particles**: Menu sparkles, selection glow

### 7.2 Screen Effects
- Screen shake: 2-15px intensity based on damage
- Hit freeze: 3-8 frame pause on heavy hits
- Slow motion: 0.5x speed during Domain startup
- Chromatic aberration: On Black Flash / critical hits
- Vignette: Darkening edges during low health

### 7.3 Character Animation (Procedural)
- Idle: Subtle breathing bob (2px)
- Walk: Leg alternation via sine wave
- Jump: Squash on takeoff, stretch in air
- Attack: Limb extension via polygon manipulation
- Hit: Flash white 2 frames, knockback shake
